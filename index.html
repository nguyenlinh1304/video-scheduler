Video Scheduler

Video Scheduler is a tool that a creator runs on their own computer to schedule and upload their own videos to their own TikTok account, using TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API.

How it works
  • Connect your TikTok account with "Login with TikTok".
  • Choose your videos, write captions and pick a time.
  • Videos are sent to your TikTok inbox as drafts, or posted directly with the privacy and interaction settings you choose.
