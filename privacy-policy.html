Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Video Scheduler is a personal tool that its owner uses to schedule and upload videos to the owner's own TikTok accounts. It is not offered to the public.

1. Information we collect

When a TikTok account is connected through "Login with TikTok", the app receives:

  • Your TikTok open ID, display name and profile picture (scope: user.info.basic)
  • Access and refresh tokens that allow the app to upload videos to that account (scopes: video.upload, video.publish)

We do not collect your TikTok password, contacts, messages, location or any other data.

2. How we use it

The information is used only to:

  • Show which TikTok account a scheduled video will be sent to
  • Upload the videos you choose, at the time you choose, to your own TikTok account

Nothing is used for advertising, profiling or analytics.

3. Storage and security

All data is stored locally on the owner's computer. Access tokens are encrypted (AES-256-GCM). Data is not stored on any third-party server other than TikTok itself.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone.

5. Your choices and deletion

You can disconnect an account at any time by deleting it in the app, which removes its stored data and tokens. You can also revoke access in the TikTok app: Settings and privacy > Security and permissions > Apps and services permissions.
